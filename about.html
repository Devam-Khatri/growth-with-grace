OUR STORY

Growing Together.
Serving Together.

Growth with Grace Parivar brings together people from different professions and backgrounds who believe in building lasting relationships, supporting one another and contributing positively to the community. Our story is rooted in the simple belief that meaningful connections create lasting value.

WHO WE ARE

A Community Built on Trust

Growth with Grace Parivar is a community where entrepreneurs, professionals, business owners and individuals from different walks of life come together with a shared purpose. While every member brings a unique experience, we are united by common values of respect, cooperation and mutual support.

We believe that genuine relationships are built over time through regular interaction, shared experiences and a willingness to help one another. By creating an environment where members can exchange ideas, offer guidance and celebrate achievements, we encourage growth that benefits both individuals and the wider community.

Beyond professional networking, Growth with Grace Parivar promotes a culture of kindness, responsibility and service. Every gathering is an opportunity to strengthen connections, learn from one another and contribute towards a positive and supportive community.

OUR FOUNDER

Mrs. Hema Khatri

Founder, Growth with Grace Parivar

Growth with Grace Parivar was founded by Mrs. Hema Khatri with the vision of creating a community where people can build meaningful relationships based on trust, respect and shared values. Her belief has always been that lasting connections are formed when people come together with openness, sincerity and a willingness to support one another.

Educational Background

Mrs. Hema Khatri completed her graduation in Varanasi, then her under-graduation from the University of Allahabad, and later earned her B.Ed in Lucknow. After moving to Lucknow, she married Mr. Roshan Khatri in 2000, and together they have two sons.

Education & Social Service

An educator and social worker at heart, she founded Little Pearls International School in 2008, a preschool that earned a reputation for quality early childhood education and successfully served families for many years.

Her commitment to social service began even earlier with the establishment of Madhayam Samajik Sanstha, an NGO founded in 2005. Through numerous large-scale community initiatives, awareness campaigns and charitable projects, the organisation has supported underprivileged communities and impacted the lives of thousands.

Other Initiatives

In 2017, she founded Epic Events, an event management company that organises large-scale social, cultural and community events, bringing together people from diverse backgrounds every year.

Mrs. Hema Khatri is also a certified parent counsellor, guiding families on child development and parenting while continuing to promote education, community welfare and social awareness through her various initiatives.

OUR VISION

Looking Ahead

Our vision is to build a community where people from different professions and backgrounds come together with mutual respect, shared values and a genuine willingness to support one another. We aim to create an environment where meaningful relationships encourage personal development, professional growth and a stronger sense of social responsibility.

OUR MISSION

What Guides Our Work

Build Relationships

Encourage genuine connections based on trust, respect and open communication. We believe strong relationships create the foundation for lasting personal and professional growth.

Share Knowledge

Create opportunities for members to exchange ideas, experiences and practical knowledge. Learning from one another helps every member grow with confidence and purpose.

Contribute to Society

Encourage members to use their skills, experience and resources to make a positive contribution to the community through service, cooperation and responsible leadership.

OUR VALUES

The Principles That Guide Us

Integrity
We believe that honesty, transparency and accountability are essential to building relationships that last.


Respect
Every member deserves to be heard, valued and treated with courtesy, regardless of their profession, experience or background.


Collaboration
We encourage members to work together, exchange ideas and support one another for mutual benefit rather than individual success alone.


Continuous Learning
Growth comes through curiosity, experience and the willingness to learn from others. We encourage members to keep developing both personally and professionally.


Service
We believe every community becomes stronger when individuals are willing to contribute their time, knowledge and experience for the benefit of others.


Responsibility
Every member represents the values of Growth with Grace Parivar. We encourage responsible actions, ethical conduct and consideration for the wider community.
