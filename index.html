Welcome to our community

Good relationships make room for growth.

Growth with Grace Parivar brings people together beyond a single profession or industry. Our gatherings create space for conversation, collaboration and the kind of introductions that can become lasting relationships.

We value practical exchange as much as personal connection: members share experience, open doors for one another and look for thoughtful ways to serve.

Meet our founder

Mrs. Hema Khatri

Growth with Grace was founded on a simple idea: when people choose to support one another with sincerity, a stronger community follows.

Through education, social initiatives and community events, Mrs. Hema Khatri has built spaces where people can meet, learn from one another and turn relationships into meaningful action.
